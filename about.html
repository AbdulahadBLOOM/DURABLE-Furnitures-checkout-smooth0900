Who We Are

Pakistan's Most Trusted Furniture Destination

Durable Furnitures was founded in 1999 with a simple vision — to bring premium, long-lasting furniture to every Pakistani home. Over 25 years, we have grown into a multi-brand destination that houses the best names in the industry.

We partner exclusively with Durian, Godrej, Nilkamal, and HomeTown — four brands that share our commitment to craftsmanship, durability, and design excellence.
