About Durian

Luxury at Its Finest

Durian is synonymous with luxury furniture across Pakistan and South Asia. Established in 1984, they specialize in high-end leather sofas, premium bedroom sets, and elegant living room collections crafted with imported materials.

With exclusive showrooms across Pakistan and their signature craftsmanship, Durian stands for lasting elegance and uncompromised quality.
