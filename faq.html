Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products, delivery, warranty & more.

Standard delivery takes 5–10 working days across India. For Karachi & nearby areas, we offer express delivery within 2–3 days. Large furniture items like beds and sofas may require 7–14 days due to special handling.

Yes! We offer free delivery on all orders above Rs.10,000. For orders below this amount, a flat delivery charge of Rs.499 applies. Delivery charges are shown clearly at checkout before you place your order.

Absolutely! We provide free professional installation for all furniture. Our trained team will assemble and set up your furniture in your desired location. Installation is scheduled at your convenience during delivery.

We are authorised dealers for four premium Indian furniture brands: Durian, Godrej Interio, Nilkamal, and HomeTown. Each brand has its own unique range covering living room, bedroom, dining, office, and kids furniture.

Customisation options depend on the brand and product. Durian offers the widest customisation — fabric, finish, and dimensions. Godrej and Nilkamal have limited colour variants. Please contact us for specific customisation queries and lead times.

Yes! Our showroom in Andheri West, Karachi has a large display of products from all four brands. We're open Mon–Sat 10 AM–8 PM and Sundays 11 AM–6 PM. You can also book a private showroom tour — contact us to schedule one.

All products come with the official brand warranty. Durian offers up to 5 years on frames, Godrej Interio provides 1–3 years, and Nilkamal gives 1–2 years depending on the product. Warranty covers manufacturing defects but excludes damage from misuse.

To claim warranty, simply WhatsApp or call us at +92 300 1234567 with your order number and photos of the defect. We will coordinate with the brand's service team and schedule a visit within 48–72 hours at no charge to you.

We accept all major payment methods — UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, EMI, and Cash on Delivery (for orders up to Rs.25,000). No-cost EMI options are available on select credit cards with 3, 6, and 12 month plans.

Yes! We run seasonal sales and festive offers (Diwali, New Year, etc). Subscribe to our newsletter or follow us on Instagram to get early access. Bulk orders (3+ items) also get an additional 5–10% discount — contact us for a custom quote.

We offer a 7-day return policy from delivery date for damaged or defective items. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Custom-made or personalised orders are non-returnable. Contact us within 7 days with photos to initiate a return.

Once your return is approved, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method. UPI and wallet refunds are typically faster (1–3 days). You will receive an email confirmation once the refund is initiated.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to help you with anything — from product advice to order tracking.
